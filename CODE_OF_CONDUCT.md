# Code of Conduct

OpenDiffs is committed to a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate of different backgrounds, experience levels, and communication styles.
- Discuss ideas and code on their merits; give specific, actionable feedback.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and project boundaries.
- Accept maintainers' decisions and correction gracefully.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not tolerated in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Include relevant links or context. Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential to the extent possible.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant when behavior harms the community. Decisions will consider context, severity, repetition, and the safety of affected people.

This policy applies to repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces managed by OpenDiffs.
